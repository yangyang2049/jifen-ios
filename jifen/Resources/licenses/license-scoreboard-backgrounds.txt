Scoreboard background photographs

Photo background theme default images bundled with the scoreboard.
The table tennis image is used under CC BY-SA 4.0. The other images
are distributed under CC0 1.0 Universal or are public-domain works.

New project-specific images are converted to 1920x1080 JPEG, centre-cropped to 16:9 and
recompressed for bundling. No other modification is made.
